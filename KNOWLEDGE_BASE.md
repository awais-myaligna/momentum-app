## What Momentum Is

Momentum is an emotional awareness app. It helps people build a clearer, ongoing picture of how they're feeling — not through a mood-tracking survey, but through short, natural, conversational check-ins guided by voice.

Momentum is not about fixing feelings or producing a single "score" to chase. It's about noticing patterns over time: which parts of someone's emotional life are steady, which are shifting, and where a little more attention might help. Momentum is part of the broader **Aligna** family of apps; with the user's permission, trends noticed in Momentum can help other Aligna experiences (such as Lotus and TargetPro) offer more relevant, personalized support.

Momentum is a self-awareness tool, not a medical, psychiatric, or crisis service. It does not diagnose conditions, treat mental health issues, or replace a licensed professional.

## The 12 Core Emotions

Momentum tracks the same 12 emotions consistently, so a person's picture over time is comparable and meaningful:

1. **Joy** — happiness, lightness, and fulfillment from positive moments, progress, or simple daily experiences.
2. **Gratitude** — recognition and appreciation of the good in life: people, experiences, opportunities, or small moments of beauty.
3. **Inspiration** — the spark that fuels creativity, motivation, and possibility; connection to ideas or people that move someone toward what they care about.
4. **Focus** — the ability to direct attention and energy toward what matters most; staying present and aligned with priorities.
5. **Determination** — the inner resolve to keep moving forward through challenges; commitment, discipline, and persistence.
6. **Compassion** — the ability to understand and care about others' experiences; kindness, empathy, and connection.
7. **Clarity** — a sense of understanding one's path or next step, reducing confusion.
8. **Confidence** — belief in one's own abilities, worth, and capacity to act; speaking up, deciding, and pursuing goals with self-assurance.
9. **Enthusiasm** — energetic excitement and positive anticipation toward tasks, opportunities, or what's ahead.
10. **Resilience** — the ability to recover from setbacks, adapt to challenges, and stay emotionally strong through difficulty.
11. **Hope** — the optimistic belief that positive outcomes are possible, even when the path isn't fully clear yet.
12. **Contentment** — calm satisfaction with where one is right now, without constantly needing something more.

These were chosen because together they represent a broad picture of emotional alignment and personal growth — not just "good mood vs. bad mood," but the different dimensions that make up how someone is really doing.

## The Assessment Process (Baseline "Emotional Compass Check-In")

The first time someone uses Momentum, after a short introduction to the 12 emotions, they complete a one-time **baseline check-in**. This establishes their starting point.

- The baseline walks through **all 12 emotions, one at a time**, in a fixed order.
- For each emotion, the user simply has a short, spoken reflective conversation about it, guided by Momentum's AI. There's nothing to rate or select — the AI listens to what the user shares and determines the score for that emotion, from **1 to 10**, based on the conversation itself.
- There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is honesty in the moment, not a "good" result.
- Once all 12 are rated, the user sees their first chart — a color-coded snapshot of where they stand across all 12 emotions today. This baseline is described to users as "only a starting point," not a verdict.
- After the baseline, users see a short explainer (the "Emotional Roadmap") describing how ongoing daily check-ins work, before landing on their main dashboard.

## Daily Check-Ins

After the baseline, Momentum keeps the picture current with short **daily check-ins**:

- Each day, Momentum checks in on **4 of the 12 emotions** — not all 12, to keep it light.
- The 12 emotions are split into three fixed groups of four, and the app rotates through these groups on a repeating **3-day cycle**:
  - Group A: Joy, Gratitude, Inspiration, Focus
  - Group B: Determination, Compassion, Clarity, Confidence
  - Group C: Enthusiasm, Resilience, Hope, Contentment
- Every 3 days, all 12 emotions have been revisited once. Each time a group comes back around, the reflective question for each emotion is refreshed with a new variant, so check-ins don't feel repetitive.
- A daily check-in only takes a couple of minutes.
- Missing a day is completely fine. There's no streak to lose or penalty for gaps — the user simply picks up where they left off whenever they return. Consistency over time matters far more than never missing a day.

## Charts and Scores

- Every emotion is scored on a simple **1 to 10 scale**, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high, reflecting how the person feels right now — not a lifetime average.
- The user never chooses this number themselves. Momentum's AI determines it by listening to how they describe each emotion in conversation — the scale exists to make the result easy to read on a chart, not as something to fill out.
- Scores are color-coded on Momentum's charts to make patterns easy to see at a glance:
  - **Red** — lower scores, meaning this emotion may need more attention or support right now.
  - **Yellow/Orange** — mid-range scores, meaning things are building or holding steady.
  - **Green** — higher scores, meaning this emotion is currently thriving.
- A **lower score is not something to feel bad about.** It simply highlights where a person might benefit from more awareness, attention, or support — the same way a low battery icon just tells you it's time to recharge, not that something is wrong with the phone.
- Users can view their full chart across all 12 emotions at any time, tap into any single emotion for a closer look (their current score plus a short, encouraging reflection tailored to that score range), and browse their History to see every past check-in (baseline and daily) over time.
- Momentum's "Emotional Roadmap" explains the ongoing check-in cycle using a GPS analogy: the baseline is the starting point, and each check-in helps Momentum help the user navigate their emotional journey step by step.

## Feature Guide

- **Home** — a quick daily summary: overall average, current check-in day, and the emotions currently most worth focusing on.
- **Chart** — the full color-coded view of all 12 emotions, with the ability to tap into any one for detail.
- **Check-In** — where the day's 4-emotion reflection happens.
- **History** — a timeline of every completed check-in (baseline and daily), each with its date and scores.
- **Profile** — account details and preferences, including preferred language, preferred voice, and daily check-in reminder notifications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does the daily check-in work?**
After the baseline, Momentum checks in on four emotions per day in a rotating 3-day cycle. Each time a group comes back around, there's a fresh question, so all 12 emotions are revisited every 3 days.

**What if I miss a day?**
That's okay. Daily check-ins only take a couple of minutes, but consistency matters more than perfection. Users simply pick up where they left off whenever they return.

**What do the chart colors mean?**
Lower scores appear in red, mid-range scores in yellow or orange, and higher scores in green. Lower scores aren't something to fear — they simply show where someone may benefit from more awareness, attention, or support.

**How is my data used?**
Emotional data is stored in the user's shared Aligna profile, accessible across the Aligna suite, so trends noticed in Momentum can help other Aligna apps offer more relevant, personalized support.

**Is Momentum a substitute for therapy or medical care?**
No. Momentum is a self-awareness and reflection tool. It is not a diagnostic, medical, psychiatric, or crisis service, and it does not replace a licensed professional. If someone is in crisis or needs urgent support, they should be encouraged to contact a mental health professional or emergency services directly.

## User Guidance

- There's no "wrong" way to answer a check-in — honesty in the moment is the only goal.
- Scores are personal and private to the user's own picture; they are not compared against other people.
- The point of Momentum is long-term pattern awareness, not any single day's result. One low day (or one low score) is just one data point, not a trend.
- Users can change their preferred language, preferred voice, and notification settings at any time from their Profile.
